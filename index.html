    Support & App

    How do I add the front and back?

    Choose one photo that shows both cards and the app splits them automatically. Or add the front first, then open its Back tab and add the photo of the back.

    The outline is not on my document

    Tap Re-analyse to try another way of finding the edges. If it is still off, tap Edit crop and drag the round corner handles or edge handles. Use the Loupe toggle right beside Edit crop to show or hide the zoom magnifier, and tap Done when finished.

    Why can I not move the crop?

    Crops are locked by default so touching the photo while scrolling never changes them. Tap Edit crop to unlock it, and toggle Scroll lock directly on the document card if you want crops to stay unlocked while scrolling.

    How do I print at the real size?

    Create the file and tap Print in the preview, or download the PDF and print it. In the print dialog choose the same paper size you picked here and set scale to 100% or Actual size. Turn off Fit to page, or the documents will print larger or smaller.

    Which size will my document be?

    Auto-detect chooses an ID card or an A4 page from the shape of the document. If it guesses wrong, pick the right type under Choose the output. Sizes for the old voter ID and bank passbook are approximate, so use Custom width × height if yours differs.

    Where are my files stored?

    Only on this device, in this browser. Nothing is uploaded. Clearing your browser data removes the saved documents and your profile.
